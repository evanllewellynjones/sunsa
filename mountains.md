## Atmosphere & depth

- **Aerial perspective:** each range further back gets lighter, bluer and less saturated, with less contrast
- Paint back to front — sky, far ranges, middle ranges, foreground
- Keep the most distant range almost the sky color plus a touch of blue-violet

## Form & light

- Decide where the sun is first; every peak has a consistent lit plane and shadow plane
- Snow in sun: warm white (white + a touch of yellow/orange); snow in shadow: blue-violet, never grey
- Use the side of a flat brush or palette knife to drag rock planes — follow the slope direction
- Vary the ridgeline — avoid evenly spaced peaks and symmetrical "camel humps"

## Base of the mountain

- Soften the base into mist or tree line so the mountain doesn't look pasted on
- Tree lines in the distance are just a dark, cool band with a broken top edge
