## Reflections

- Reflections fall **straight down**, directly beneath the object, regardless of where the light comes from
- Reflections of light objects are slightly darker; reflections of dark objects are slightly lighter
- Pull the reflection down with vertical strokes, then add a few **horizontal** strokes across it to make it read as water

## Color & surface

- Water mirrors the sky — check the sky color before mixing water
- Calm water: smooth blending, soft edges. Moving water: broken strokes, harder edges
- Water gets lighter toward the horizon (reflecting more sky at a low angle)

## Details

- Ripple lines get smaller and closer together as they recede
- A few bright sparkles or a thin light line along the far shore makes water read instantly — use sparingly
- Add highlights last, and keep them horizontal
