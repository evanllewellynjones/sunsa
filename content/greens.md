## Creating & using greens

Tube greens are too saturated for natural foliage. Seven rules for mixing greens that look real:

1. **Always de-saturate** — red de-saturates green.
2. **Darken** — blue darkens green directly; red darkens it and makes it warmer.
3. **Lighten with yellow** — then add a touch of red to warm it again.
4. **Use white sparingly** — it chalks greens quickly.
5. **Temperature** — overall, blue makes a green cooler; red or yellow warms it.
6. **Atmospheric perspective**
   - As things get further away, first the yellows drop out, then the reds — you're left with blue / cooler greens.
   - The further back you go, the more you lighten the green with white or yellow.
7. **Shortcut colors**
   - **Sap Green** — the starting color.
   - **Yellow Ochre + blue** — for trees in the distance.
