## Building the form

- Think of rocks as **blocks with planes** — top (lightest), side facing the light, shadow side
- Block in the dark shadow shape first, then mid-tone, then lights on top
- Use a palette knife or the edge of a flat brush for crisp, angular edges

## Color

- Rocks reflect their surroundings: blue-violet from the sky in shadow, green near foliage, warm near sand
- Mix greys from complements (Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine) rather than black + white
- Add small cracks and crevices only in the light areas and near the focal point

## Grounding

- Give every rock a dark contact shadow where it meets the ground or water so it doesn't float
- Vary sizes — one large, a few medium, many small — and overlap them
- Rocks in water: reflection is darker and slightly broken by ripples
