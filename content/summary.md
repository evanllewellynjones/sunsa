## Layout \& Info

* **First:** Sketch out on a separate piece of paper the general composition and where TONAL VALUES will be — try to define where 5–9 tonal values, from light to dark, are represented in the painting

  * Consider foreground, mid ground, background, and focal point in the beginning
* **Second**: Do an UNDERPAINTING with a warm neutral color (Burnt Sienna) entire canvas and TONAL VALUES

  * Use it to Underpainting to block out areas based on dark and light values
  * 5–15 areas that are light vs. dark in the painting
* **Third:** Start with dark values

  * Always at least 3 layers: dark, mid-tone and bright

## Mistakes to be Avoided

* **Less detail overall** — use larger brushes; small brushes force detail; much less detail in background
* **Color harmony:** don't use too many colors
* Use highlights sparingly
* Don't use straight black — use blue, purple, brown
* Don't use straight white — use yellow, light blue
* **No symmetry:** objects not in a pattern; don't put the horizon right in the middle

## Technique

* Spray water on paints more
* Use medium more
* Think about where paint is very watery vs. solid
* Highlight over highlights to make them pop — letting the first layer show through a little creates distinction between *sun* and *very sunny* in different areas
* Sky holes: darker, more neutral, variety of size and placement
* Use red to make colors warm and blue to make them cool
