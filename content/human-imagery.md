## Starting the figure

- Start with the **gesture** — one quick line for the spine/action before any outline
- Measure in heads: an adult is roughly **7½–8 heads** tall; the halfway point is the hips
- Block the figure as simple shapes (egg for the head, box for the ribcage, box for the pelvis) before any detail
- Find the **light side vs. shadow side** first — two values, then refine

## Skin & color

- Skin is never one color: warmer (reds, oranges) where blood is close — cheeks, nose, ears, knuckles; cooler (greens, blues) at the jaw and in shadow
- Shadows on skin: mix the base tone with its complement or a touch of Burnt Umber + Ultramarine — not black
- Keep highlights small and slightly cool

## Figures in a scene

- Distant figures are just a few strokes: a vertical body shape, a small dot of a head, no feet
- Heads in a crowd line up on the horizon line when the ground is flat and the viewer is standing
- Keep edges **soft** everywhere except one or two places you want the eye to go
- Leave features (eyes, mouth) for last, and use less detail than you think
