## Shape first

- Paint the tree's overall **silhouette shape** before any leaves — every species has a characteristic shape
- Leave **sky holes** in the canopy — darker and more neutral than the open sky, varied in size and placement
- Branches taper and get thinner toward the tips; they rarely come straight at you

## Value & color

- Three values minimum: dark interior, mid-tone mass, sunlit clumps on the light side
- Clump the foliage — paint groups of leaves as shapes, not individual leaves
- Mix greens rather than using tube green straight; tone down with red or Burnt Sienna
- Distant trees: cooler, bluer and lighter; foreground trees: warmer and more detailed

## Technique

- A fan brush or old splayed brush, dabbed (not stroked), for foliage texture
- Trunks are not brown: grey, violet, green moss tones; lighter on the sunny side
- Soft edges where foliage meets sky in the background; crisper edges in the foreground
