## Sand

- Sand is not beige: warm ochre/Naples Yellow in sun, lavender/blue-grey in shadow
- Wet sand near the water is darker and reflects the sky — treat it like a dull mirror
- Add texture with a dry brush or spatter, mostly in the foreground

## Water & surf

- The sea gets darker and bluer toward the horizon; lighter and greener in the shallows
- Keep the horizon **perfectly level** — a tilted horizon ruins a beach painting
- Surf foam: paint the shadow side of the foam first (blue-grey), then add whites only on top where light hits
- Waves are mostly horizontal shapes; small strokes, wider in front, thinner in the distance

## Composition

- Put the horizon high or low — not in the middle
- Use dunes, grasses or a path as a lead-in line from the foreground
- Dune grass: quick upward flicks with a liner brush, varied heights and angles
