## Layout & Info

- Sketch out very roughly
- Block out areas based on dark and light values
  - 8–15 areas that are light vs. dark in the painting
- Consider foreground, mid ground, background, and focal point in the beginning
  - Less detail in background
- Use highlights sparingly
- Don't use straight black — use blue, purple, brown
- Don't use straight white — use yellow, light blue
- Use red to make colors warm and blue to make them cool
- Highlight over highlights to make them pop — letting the first layer show through a little creates distinction between *sun* and *very sunny* in different areas
- Always at least 3 layers: dark, mid-tone and bright
- Sky holes: darker, more neutral, variety of size and placement

## Technique

- Spray water on paints more
- Use medium more
- Think about where paint is very watery vs. solid
